About us

We work with producers and agricultural businesses across the United States and in four countries, helping them manage price risk and keep watch on the markets.

Our team

Our brokers bring experience in livestock, grains, softs, and across the commercial grain industry. From processors to shuttle loaders, importers and exporters, we've got you covered.

Our process

We offer an independent view of the markets. Perspective on what is truly driving price is something we constantly strive for. The world we live in changes every day, and we think the way we look at markets should keep up with it.
